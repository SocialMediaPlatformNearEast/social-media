LvL Guide

Earn XP, unlock profile style, and make your progress visible without reading a rule book.

🎮 Progress system

Use LvL like a social app. Level up like a game.

Posting, replying, liking, messaging, and high-fiving all feed your XP track. Higher LvLs unlock style rewards that make your profile feel earned.

⚡ Score track XP → LvL → Rewards

Achievements are display badges for now. They show progress, but they do not add bonus XP.

How it works

Three simple steps: interact, fill the bar, unlock a visible reward.

💬 1. Join in

Post, reply, like, repost, message, or high-five someone.

⚡ 2. Gain XP

Useful activity fills your LvL bar and helps your profile grow.

🏅 3. Show rewards

Unlock badges, borders, colors, titles, and future icon recolors.
