Progress system

Every useful social action builds your public score.

XP adds to your total score. When your total reaches the next requirement, your LvL goes up and your profile unlocks stronger status visuals.

Score track XP → LvL → Rewards

Achievements are display badges for now. They show progress, but they do not add bonus XP.
